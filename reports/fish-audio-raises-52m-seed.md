## The Agency Play
- **What to build:** Partial functionality: branded voice content engines (audiobooks, game NPCs, ad reads, IVR personas) on commodity TTS APIs with custom voice design and human QA - Fish Audio's use cases without training models.
- **How to package it:** Productized 'Brand Voice Kit' (design + library + integration) then per-campaign production retainer.
- **Suggested pricing:** Kit $8-20k; production retainer $2.5-8k/mo; per-minute overages.
- **First clients:** Mid-size game studios, e-learning platforms and performance-marketing agencies needing volume voice at scale.
