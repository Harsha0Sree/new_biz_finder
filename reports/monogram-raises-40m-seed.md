## The Agency Play
- **What to build:** Partial functionality: generative-UI overlays for specific enterprise workflows (analytics dashboards that assemble themselves per question, config tools) on FM APIs - Monogram's interaction model applied to B2B software.
- **How to package it:** Fixed-scope 'Conversational Interface' pilot on one product surface; iteration retainer.
- **Suggested pricing:** Pilot $20-45k; retainer $3-6k/mo.
- **First clients:** B2B SaaS products whose users abandon complex configuration UIs - visible via support-ticket themes.
