## The Agency Play
- **What to build:** Partial functionality: AgentOps-as-a-service - deploy, observe, and govern client AI-agent fleets using OSS tracing/eval stacks; Sycamore's lifecycle without building the OS.
- **How to package it:** 'Agent Ops Starter' (inventory + tracing + guardrails) fixed scope; monthly managed-AgentOps retainer scaling with fleet size.
- **Suggested pricing:** Starter $30-60k; retainer $5-12k/mo; per-agent onboarding $5-10k.
- **First clients:** Series B+ SaaS and fintechs running 5+ production agents with no observability or eval discipline.
