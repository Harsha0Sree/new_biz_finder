## The Agency Play
- **What to build:** Partial functionality strictly below the clinical line: HIPAA-compliant administrative agents (scheduling, refill logistics, prep instructions) on FM APIs with BAAs and human escalation - Hippocratic's operational periphery without patient-clinical judgment.
- **How to package it:** Fixed-scope 'Front-Office Agent' pilot per specialty; compliance-evidence pack add-on; managed retainer with audit logs.
- **Suggested pricing:** Pilot $40-80k; retainer $5-10k/mo; refuse any diagnostic/triage scope.
- **First clients:** Mid-size multi-site clinics and dental/derm groups with front-desk staffing shortages.
