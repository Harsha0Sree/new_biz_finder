## The Agency Play
- **What to build:** Partial functionality: vertical voice agents (booking, intake, order-status) on commodity STT-LLM-TTS stacks with telephony - replicating Gradium's use cases while they build the frontier model underneath.
- **How to package it:** Productized 'AI Receptionist' per vertical with setup fee + per-minute hosting; multilingual and after-hours add-ons.
- **Suggested pricing:** Setup $10-30k; $500-2k/mo managed hosting+optimization; multi-location programs $50k+.
- **First clients:** Dental/medical groups, home services, restaurants chains - high call volumes, staff shortage pain, demo-ready.
