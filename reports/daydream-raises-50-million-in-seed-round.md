## The Agency Play
- **What to build:** Partial functionality: AI product-discovery layers for mid-market fashion/lifestyle retailers - semantic search, visual 'shop the look', personalized feeds on Algolia/VLM APIs - Daydream's UX as a retailer service.
- **How to package it:** Fixed-scope 'Discovery Upgrade' per storefront; seasonal merchandising retainer.
- **Suggested pricing:** Build $25-60k; retainer $3-7k/mo.
- **First clients:** $10-100M DTC fashion/beauty brands with large catalogs and weak on-site search.
