## The Agency Play
- **What to build:** Partial functionality: automated sound-design service for game/film/social studios - SFX and ambience layers generated via commodity audio APIs with human curation, integrated into client pipelines - Mirelo's use cases without training models.
- **How to package it:** Productized 'Sound Layer Kit' per genre; per-project production retainer with SLA turnaround.
- **Suggested pricing:** Kit $10-25k; production retainer $3-8k/mo.
- **First clients:** Indie game studios and social/content agencies shipping volume video without dedicated audio staff.
