## The Agency Play
- **What to build:** Partial functionality: AI support agents for mid-market companies covering top ticket categories, wired into their existing helpdesk with escalation rules and KB-sync loops - Decagon's product as a managed service.
- **How to package it:** Productized 'Support Agent Launch': knowledge ingestion + agent build + 2-week supervised run; then per-resolution pricing.
- **Suggested pricing:** Launch $20-45k; $0.80-2.50/resolution managed; floor retainer $2.5-6k/mo.
- **First clients:** Consumer SaaS and e-commerce brands with 5-50 person support teams and rising ticket volume.
