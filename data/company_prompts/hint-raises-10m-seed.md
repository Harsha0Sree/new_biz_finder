Hint Raises $10M Seed
Hint, a Charlotte, N.C.-based company creating an AI-native home management platform to help homeowners manage and optimize their homes, announced it has raised $10 million in seed funding.
Investors
This seed round was led by Slow Ventures. Additional participating investors included Montauk Capital, Tusk Venture Partners, Amplo, Energy Impact Partners, Hannah Grey VC, and Brian Kelly, founder of The Points Guy.
Hint Use of Funds
The capital raised will support the launch and development of Hint's AI-native home management platform. The company aims to empower homeowners by providing personalized, proactive guidance to navigate complex decisions, manage essential tasks, reduce costs, and enhance long-term home value. The funding will enable Hint to bring its platform to market, with availability on desktop and iOS planned for summer 2026.
About Hint
Hint is creating the future of home management to unlock every home’s full potential. Founded in 2026 by Martha Stewart, Yih-Han Ma, and Kyle Rush, Hint is an always-on, AI-native home management platform that serves as a single, trusted interface to manage and optimize every facet of the home. The company’s mission is to empower homeowners to take control of their homes and make homeownership more affordable for all.
