NEW YORK
,
June 10, 2026
/PRNewswire/ -- Dapple,
the operating system for AI infrastructure
, today announced the close of a $30 million seed funding round backed by The Raptor Group and Ion Pacific. Five months from launch, Dapple has enterprise customers running production AI workloads in dedicated, single-tenant environments. The capital will accelerate global deployments and the expansion of the new category of AI infrastructure created by Dapple, the Enterprise OS Cloud.
Enterprises that require single-tenant capacity, deterministic performance, data residency and auditable governance have historically had only three options: spend years building it in-house, wait for limited hyperscaler capacity or rent raw compute without enterprise controls. Dapple has introduced a fourth option. The Enterprise OS Cloud is an operating system that sits above the fragmented stack and turns it into a single, governed deployment.
"For years, the enterprises with the most to deploy on AI had no good place to run it. The Enterprise OS Cloud ends that choice," said
Tricia Martinez
, Chief Executive Officer, Dapple. "We did not raise capital to build what exists today. We are building the next generation of enterprise AI infrastructure with over $100M in contracts to prove it. This new capital will help us scale exponentially."
The Enterprise OS Cloud is built around dedicated, in-country cloud environments, giving organizations more control over their data, stronger security
,
and predictable performance. The infrastructure is live today and carrying production AI workloads globally.
"We have backed category creators before. Dapple did the rare thing. It proved the category in production before it raised funds to scale. More than $100M in customer contracts in five months of operations is a market telling you it was waiting for this," said Jim Pallotta, Managing Director and Chairman, Raptor Group.
"The demand we see is global. Enterprises everywhere want to run AI inside their own borders, on infrastructure they control. " We are not aware of anyone else who can effectively deliver this," said
Michael Joseph
, Founding Partner, Ion Pacific. "Dapple can, and it is already live. That is why we backed this round."
Demand for AI infrastructure has outpaced available supply. Enterprises wanting hundreds or thousands of accelerators sit behind frontier-model customers consuming tens of thousands. The infrastructure exists, but not in a form most enterprises can deploy or operate at scale. Dapple closes that gap. The Enterprise OS Cloud puts dedicated, in-country infrastructure within reach of every enterprise, not only the largest model builders.
"Enterprises need infrastructure built for their own workloads, not a shared system that they rent by the hour. We built it dedicated and in-country for the individual enterprise, and it is carrying production load today," said
Salam Al-Mosawi
, Chief Operating Officer, Dapple.
ABOUT DAPPLE
Dapple created the Enterprise OS Cloud, a new category of AI infrastructure that sits between the public cloud and the private data center. Dedicated, in-country, single-tenant AI infrastructure for regulated enterprises and hypergrowth digital natives that cannot run production AI on shared cloud. Dapple was founded by Tricia Martinez and Salam Al-Mosawi, whose teams have collectively deployed over 300,000 accelerators across enterprise and hyperscaler environments.

Building the
Enterprise OS Cloud.
Two years ago we kept hearing the same story. The institutions doing the most important work in the world had nowhere to run the AI workloads that mattered most.
Why we built this.
01
The problem
Public cloud was built for elasticity and shared tenancy. That is the wrong shape for a bank, an insurer, a government agency, or any business that cannot put its most sensitive workloads on infrastructure it shares with strangers. The alternative was to build a private data center. That takes years and hundreds of millions in capital. By the time it is live, the model you wanted to train has already moved on.
02
The gap
So the businesses that needed production AI most were stuck between a public cloud that could not isolate them and a private build that could not move fast enough. That gap is where the category lives.
03
What we built
Dapple built what fills the gap.
The Enterprise OS Cloud is a new category of AI infrastructure. Single-tenant by architecture. In‑country by physical boundary. Built for production AI in regulated industries and AI-native companies that cannot run on shared infrastructure.

Leadership.
Tricia Martinez
Founder & CEO
At Dapple
Tricia Martinez is the Founder and CEO of Dapple, a technology entrepreneur, investor, and operator building the next generation of enterprise AI infrastructure. Dapple builds and operates dedicated, single-tenant AI infrastructure for regulated enterprises, governments, and AI-native companies, delivered as a fully managed Enterprise OS Cloud through a single environment, interface, and contract. Under her leadership, Dapple has rapidly emerged as a strategic partner to hyperscalers, OEMs, enterprises, and governments seeking scalable, private, and compliant AI cloud infrastructure globally.
Experience
Prior to Dapple, Tricia founded and scaled Africa's first digital bank, leading the company through rapid growth and acquisition. She later served as a White House Fellow at the U.S. Department of Energy, where she worked across AI, energy, and national infrastructure initiatives involving the U.S. national laboratory system. She also led six accelerator programs at Techstars in partnership with organizations including JPMorgan, NVIDIA, and the Department of Energy. Tricia is an active investor in more than 80 emerging technology companies and is known for operating at the intersection of AI, infrastructure, finance, and geopolitics.
Salam Al-Mosawi
Founder & COO
At Dapple
Salam (Sal) Al-Mosawi is the Chief Operating Officer of Dapple, a commercial operator and category builder with deep experience scaling AI and cloud compute ventures. Dapple builds and operates dedicated, single-tenant AI infrastructure for regulated enterprises, governments, and AI-native companies, delivered as a fully managed Enterprise OS Cloud through a single environment, interface, and contract. At Dapple, Salam leads data center strategy, GPU deployment, go-to-market, category design, and partnerships, building the commercial engine and ecosystem that turn Dapple's technical capability into global momentum across Europe, the US, APAC, and the GCC.
Experience
Prior to Dapple, Salam served as Chief Commercial Officer at FlexAI and SVP of Growth & Partnerships at Nscale, where his teams sold tens of thousands of GPUs globally. His career spans two Series A rounds, an IPO, and multiple acquisitions, with earlier leadership roles at Domino Data Lab, Peak, and ZAP. He began his career in data science and analytics, building decision systems for global organizations before shifting to the infrastructure and strategy that scale them.
