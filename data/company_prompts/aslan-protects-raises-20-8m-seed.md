Aslan Protects
, a Washington, D.C.-based defense technology and cyber intelligence startup, raised $20.8m in seed funding.
The round was co-led by Khosla Ventures and XYZ Venture Capital, with participation from BoxGroup, 2048 Ventures, Liquid 2 Ventures, Alumni Ventures, and other defense tech backers.
The company intends to use the capital to scale its core software engineering, data science, and threat intelligence workforces, expand its high-performance computational infrastructure, and accelerate the deployment of its digital operatives across American national security, intelligence, and federal law enforcement agencies.
Founded in 2025, Aslan develops a specialized cyber defense and intelligence platform that deploys human-supervised, autonomous AI operatives designed to infiltrate hostile digital environments at internet scale. Moving beyond static data collection and information feeds, the software-driven operatives utilize advanced natural language processing and localized behavioral models to establish a persistent presence within encrypted criminal forums and illicit online spaces.
The platform can expose cross-border smuggling networks moving people into the United States, unmask foreign state actors targeting American citizens behind fake digital identities, and maps out international cyber-fraud syndicates, gathering real-time digital evidence while maintaining strict human oversight and operational security.
