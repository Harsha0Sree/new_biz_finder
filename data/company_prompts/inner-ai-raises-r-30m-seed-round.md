Inner AI, a Brazil-based artificial intelligence startup, has raised R$30 million in a Seed round, valuing the company at R$500 million.
Investors
The round was co-led by Canary and ONEVC, with participation from Norte Ventures, Crivo Ventures, and Phenomen VC, bringing Inner AI’s total funding to R$42 million.
Canary is a Latin America–focused venture capital firm specializing in pre-Seed and Seed-stage investments, often backing founders early and supporting them through later rounds. Founded in 2017, Canary has backed over 230 companies.
ONEVC, based in São Paulo, invests in early-stage technology startups across Latin America and provides hands-on support in hiring, strategy, and scaling. Founded in 2017, ONEVC looks for startups with strong founding teams, large market opportunities, and the potential to build category-defining products.
Inner AI Use of Funds
The company will use the funds to accelerate the launch and development of Squad.com, its autonomous AI agent platform designed to enable collaborative, always-on digital workers across business functions.
About Inner AI
Founded in 2023 by Pedro Salles and Eduardo Mitelman, Inner AI builds AI-powered tools to enhance productivity and automate business operations. Its platform integrates 50+ AI models, including ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, into a single workspace. The company’s second product, Squad.com, enables businesses to deploy autonomous, collaborative AI agents operating 24/7 across multiple departments. Headquartered in São Paulo, Brazil, Inner AI serves enterprise clients, including Embraer, Vivo, Sabesp, and Bayer.

Introducing Squad.com
From the start, Inner AI was built to be the professional's co-pilot — centralizing over 50 AI models, including ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, in a single workspace. With this new funding, we are accelerating the launch of a second product with an even greater ambition: Squad.com.
Squad.com was created to democratize something that has always been a privilege of large companies: having a team working for your business. The platform brings together AI agents that work autonomously and collaboratively, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, even when you're not at the screen.
At launch, Squad.com arrives with three agents integrated in a single interface:
Waz — a WhatsApp agent for sales and customer service. Responds to clients in real time, manages scheduling, runs automatic follow-ups, and delivers a daily summary of interactions.
Fin — a financial agent. Generates payment links within service conversations, logs expenses, and projects cash flow.
Maky — a marketing agent. Creates and schedules Instagram posts, replies to comments, runs personalized campaigns to win back customers, and turns positive reviews into digital reputation.
The three agents don't work in isolation: Maky generates the lead, Waz handles the service, and Fin processes the sale. A complete cycle that runs continuously, without disrupting the entrepreneur's routine.
What's next
With the launch of Squad.com, Inner AI now operates as a two-product company: the co-pilot for those who want to work more efficiently, and the autopilot for those who want their business to grow on its own.
