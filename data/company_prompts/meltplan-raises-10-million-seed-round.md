About MeltPlan
Founded in 2025 by Kanav Hasija and Tanmaya Kala, MeltPlan provides an AI-native pre-construction platform designed to improve decision-making before construction begins. The platform includes tools such as Melt Code for AI-powered building code research, Melt Takeoff for automated quantity takeoffs, and bid-leveling features that help contractors evaluate bids and project costs. Headquartered in Berkeley, California, MeltPlan is already collaborating with enterprise contractors, including DPR Construction and Innovo Group.

🔐 MeltPlan Security Disclosure Policy
At MeltPlan, we take the security of our systems and customer data seriously. We welcome responsible security research and coordinated vulnerability disclosure.
1. Reporting a Vulnerability
If you believe you have identified a security vulnerability affecting MeltPlan systems, please report it to:
security@meltplan.com
Please include:
A clear description of the issue
Steps to reproduce
Impact assessment (if known)
Screenshots or logs (if applicable)
Your contact information
We will acknowledge receipt within 5 business days.
2. Responsible Disclosure Guidelines
We ask that researchers:
Do not access, modify, or delete customer data.
Do not disrupt services (e.g., DoS, spam campaigns).
Do not perform social engineering.
Do not exploit the issue beyond what is necessary to demonstrate proof.
Provide reasonable time for remediation before public disclosure.
Unauthorized or disruptive testing may be considered abuse.
3. Scope
This policy applies to:
meltplan.com
Subdomains owned and operated by MeltPlan
Production systems controlled by MeltPlan
Out of scope:
Third-party services
Social engineering
Physical security testing
Automated mass scanning without coordination
Spam or email spoofing demonstrations without prior consent
4. Rewards
MeltPlan does not currently operate a public bug bounty program.
At our discretion, we may offer a goodwill token of appreciation for valid, responsibly disclosed vulnerabilities. Any such gesture:
Is entirely discretionary
Does not imply the existence of a formal bounty program
Does not create entitlement to future compensation
5. Safe Harbor
If you act in good faith and follow this policy:
We will not initiate legal action
We will not pursue civil claims related to your research
We request coordination prior to public disclosure
6. Disclosure Timeline
We aim to:
Acknowledge report: within 5 business days
Provide status update: within 14 business days
Resolve validated issues promptly based on severity
