Investors
The round was led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), with participation from Lakestar, Pit founders, and executives from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Deel, and Revolut, as well as the Stena and Lundin families.
Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) is a venture capital firm that backs entrepreneurs shaping the future through technology. Founded in 2009, the firm manages multiple funds across stages, sectors, and geographies. a16z invests in seed- to growth-stage companies across AI, bio + healthcare, consumer, crypto, enterprise, fintech, gaming, and infrastructure, as well as companies contributing to American dynamism. The firm currently manages more than $90 billion in assets under management across multiple funds.
Pit Use of Funds
The company will use the funding to scale its AI-native enterprise software platform, expand deployments across industries, and accelerate the development of custom AI-powered operational systems for enterprises.
About Pit
Led by CEO Adam Jafer, Pit is an AI-native enterprise software platform that helps companies replace spreadsheets, inboxes, and rigid SaaS tools with custom-built operational systems. Its platform includes Pit Studio, which learns company workflows and builds operational systems, and Pit Cloud, which provides enterprise-grade governance, security, and infrastructure. Headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden, Pit is already being used across logistics, telecom, ecommerce, and healthcare sectors, delivering automation, operational efficiency, and workflow optimization for enterprises.

Frequently asked questions
What is Pit?
Pit is the AI product team as a service. The platform that learns how your business works, and builds the systems that run it for you. Most enterprise teams still run on manual processes, spreadsheets, inboxes, and approval chains. Not because they want to. Because nothing existed that could replace them. Pit changes that. It automates the operational work that slows companies down, so your people can focus on the work that actually moves the business forward.
What does Pit actually build?
We build customised internal tools and automated workflows. Things like contract processing systems, campaign management tools, cross-system data reconciliation, and approval workflows. Each system is custom-built for your specific needs and integrates with your existing tools.
Who is Pit for?
Pit is for mid-to-large enterprises who have processes that require a lot of manual work.
If your team is spending significant time on repetitive work, copying data between systems, chasing approvals, reconciling information across tools, Pit can solve that with custom systems designed around how your work actually gets done. That might mean automations running quietly in the background between your existing systems, or an interface that fills the gaps between them. Often both.
Do I need technical expertise to use Pit?
No. You only need to be an expert in what you do, and Pit will provide the technical expertise. The person experiencing the process problem is the one defining the solution. Pit asks the right questions and handles everything else.
