About Chamelio
Founded by Alex Zilberman, Gil Banyas, and Gal Lellouche, Chamelio is an AI-driven legal intelligence platform designed to unify in-house legal workflows and replace fragmented tools with a single, connected system. It uses AI to consolidate legal processes, orchestrating everything from drafting to signing. The platform provides organizations with a single, AI-powered source of truth, supporting workflows from intake through post-signature management. Chamelio serves approximately 100 clients, including high-growth startups and large enterprises such as Wiz, Socure, Lightricks, Integrity, Global-e, Cellebrite, and Fiverr.
