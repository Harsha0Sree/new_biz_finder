FAQ
Frequently asked questions
What does Juno Advisor do?
Advisor is your tax planning sidekick. It answers complex tax questions with fact-checked, cited responses. It creates and compares planning scenarios using your client's actual data. It generates tax projections with summaries. And it drafts client-ready deliverables: emails, planning summaries, blog posts, and one-click presentation decks. It handles the research and the output so you can focus on the strategy.
Can Advisor generate a client presentation deck with one click?
Yes. Once you've run a planning scenario or tax projection, Advisor generates a client-ready slide presentation with one click. No switching to PowerPoint, no copying numbers into a template, no spending 45 minutes formatting slides after the real work is already done. The deck is built from your client's actual data and analysis, ready to present or email. You review it, make any edits you want, and deliver.
Are Advisor's answers actually fact-checked, or am I trusting a black box?
Advisor provides citations with every answer. You can see where the information came from and verify it yourself. Juno shows its work, the same principle that runs through Preparer and Reviewer.
Can Advisor draft client-facing communications?
Yes. Advisor can draft client emails, tax planning summaries, blog posts, and even slide presentations. The content is built on your client's actual data and tax expertise, not generic templates. You review and edit before anything goes out, but the heavy lifting of the first draft is done.
Does Advisor work with both 1040s and business returns?
Yes. Advisor handles individual and business return analysis, including 1120, 1120-S, and 1065. Whether a client is asking about Roth conversion scenarios on their personal return or entity selection for a new partnership, Advisor can build the planning scenarios.
How is Advisor different from using ChatGPT or Google for tax research?
Three things. First, Advisor uses your client's actual return data to generate specific, cited answers, not generic summaries. Second, it understands tax forms, planning scenarios, and regulatory context at a depth a general tool can't match. Third, your client data stays protected: Juno is SOC 2 certified, IRS Section 7216 compliant, PII is anonymized before any query hits an external system, and Juno never trains on your data. A general chatbot can't promise any of that.
Is Advisor included in my Juno plan, or is it a separate cost?
Included. Every Juno plan comes with Advisor alongside Preparer, Binder, and Reviewer. No feature tiers, no add-ons.

FAQ
Frequently asked questions
What is Juno Binder and what does it generate?
Binder is a feature inside Juno that automatically builds a structured workpaper from your uploaded client documents. It organizes everything in tax return order so your preparer can start working immediately instead of spending 20 minutes sorting PDFs. Every data point in the workpaper links back to the source doc it came from.
Does Binder replace my current workpaper software?
It can. Binder includes tick marks, calculator tapes, references, sign-offs, and all the annotation tools you'd expect from a standalone workpaper product. The difference is it lives inside Juno's core workflow, so you're not juggling a separate login, a separate file structure, or a separate subscription. One workflow, one login.
How does Binder organize the documents my clients upload?
Binder automatically classifies and orders uploaded documents in tax return order. Whether the client sends 1 neatly labeled PDF or a 40-page combined upload with W-2s, 1099s, and a K-1 mixed together, Binder sorts it into a clean, structured workpaper your team can start working from.
Can my team collaborate inside Binder during prep and review?
Yes. Binder supports real-time collaboration. Preparers and reviewers can leave notes, mark up documents, flag fields for a teammate, and track what's been reviewed, all inside the same shared workspace. No more emailing PDFs back and forth or wondering which version has the latest notes.
What markup and annotation tools does Binder include?
Binder includes tax-specific tools built for how firms actually work: tick marks, calculator tapes, cross-references between source docs and return fields, sign-offs, and freeform notes. These are built directly into the document view, not layered on as a third-party add-on.
Does Binder work with Juno Reviewer?
They're designed to work together. Reviewer flags source-to-return mismatches and prior year changes; Binder gives you the annotated workpaper where your team resolves those flags. You move from flagged item to source doc to markup without switching tools.

FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Does Reviewer replace my current review process?
No. Reviewer does the mechanical first pass: source-to-return field matching, prior year comparisons, missing document checks, and inconsistencies across related forms. You do the judgment calls. Think of it as the part of review that doesn’t need a CPA, handled before it reaches your desk.
What does Juno Reviewer actually check on a tax return?
Reviewer compares source documents to return fields and flags mismatches: a W-2 wage that doesn't match the 1040, a missed state return, income landed on the wrong line, or a K-1 amount that shifted from prior year. It also runs prior year comparisons so you see what changed without re-checking every line yourself. Think of it as the first pass you'd do at 10pm, except it never gets tired and never skips a field.
Can I customize Reviewer to match my firm's specific review checklist?
Yes. Reviewer supports firm-specific review rules, what Juno calls "Gotcha checks." You define the edge cases, missed savings, or high-risk issues your firm cares about, and Reviewer runs those checks on every return. If your firm always double-checks basis on Schedule E rentals or flags missing estimated payments, you can build that into Reviewer so it catches those automatically.
Which tax software integrations does Reviewer support?
Juno integrates with Drake, Lacerte, ProConnect, CCH Axcess, and more. Reviewer works within the same Juno workflow that pushes data to your tax software, so there's no separate setup or export step. The source-to-return comparison happens inside Juno before anything gets pushed.
How does Reviewer handle team collaboration during busy season?
Reviewer lets you assign flagged fields to specific teammates, mark items as reviewed, and track progress across the return. Your team can see what's been checked, what's still open, and who's responsible for each item. It works with Binder for integrated markup, so your whole review workflow lives in one place instead of across sticky notes, emails, and spreadsheets.
How much time does Reviewer actually save per return?
Firms using Juno report saving 2+ hours per return and cutting total time per return by 50%. The review step specifically gets faster because you're looking at real exceptions, flagged mismatches and prior year changes, instead of re-checking every line from scratch.
