Reken Raises $10M Seed
Reken, a cybersecurity startup based in the United States, has emerged from stealth mode with a $10 million seed funding round. The company specializes in on-device AI security platforms designed to protect users against AI-powered phishing, deepfakes, and other forms of digital fraud.
Investors
The funding round was backed by venture firms Greycroft and FPV Ventures.
Reken Use of Funds
The company plans to use the new capital to scale the development of its Reken Private Core platform and its flagship phishing-and-fraud defense tool, Northstar, which operates directly on user devices to maintain privacy and security.
About Reken
Founded in 2024 by Shuman Ghosemajumder and Rich Griffiths, Reken aims to address the collapse of trust in online communication. The company provides a system of small, proprietary AI models that function on individual hardware, eliminating the need to transmit data to the cloud for security analysis.

The Reken Private Core is the foundation of our architecture. It
              enables a new security model that uniquely addresses the
              traditional tradeoffs between privacy vs. security and false
              positives vs. false negatives. It also addresses recent AI
              security issues by minimizing Internet-exposed attack surface and
              avoiding sending confidential data to third-party clouds or
              frontier LLMs. These benefits are just the beginning, as each
              Reken Private Core instance is also a node of the Reken Network.
The Reken Network
A new trust layer for the Internet
Applications built on the Reken Private Core platform join together to form the Reken Network. The Reken Network is a constantly improving, virtual shield around your communications. In technical terms, it is a self-assembling, zero-trust, mesh network. In terms of business impact, it uniquely protects your organization, your supply chain, and ultimately all businesses and users on the Reken Network.
Employees within the same organization on the Reken platform form a trusted network, verifying internal communications, and subjecting external communication to additional threat analysis.
The organization's ecosystem is protected when partners, suppliers, or customers also use Reken.
All organizations using Reken become part of the Reken Network, without any confidential data exchange or additional configuration between them required.
