Aranya Raises $11M in Funding
San Francisco based Aranya, an AI infrastructure software company, has announced a total of $11 million in funding. The capital comprises a $9 million seed round and a $2 million pre-seed round to support the scaling of its AI-native multicluster operating system.
Investors
The $9 million seed round was led by First Round Capital, with participation from Box Group, Vermilion Cliffs, and Asylum Ventures. The $2 million pre-seed round was led by Asylum Ventures, with participation from Founder Collective, Parable VC, and Uncommon Ventures.
Aranya Use of Funds
Aranya intends to use the capital to expand its engineering and go-to-market teams. The funds will support the deployment of its clusterdOS technology, the launch of a new AI-native multicluster interface, and the cultivation of the open-source community around its platform.
About Aranya
Founded in 2026 by Christian Bhatia Ondaatje, Sasivarnan Kanaghasalam Sathyapriya, and Aryamika Bhatia Ondaatje, Aranya provides an open-source distributed operating system engine built on Kubernetes. The platform converts raw bare metal servers into self-healing, production-ready clusters in under 48 hours, allowing teams to manage complex AI infrastructure using natural language control.

Frequently asked questions
What is Aranya?
Aranya is the multicluster operating system that turns any bare metal into custom production-ready clusters in 48 hours. We automate all of the moving parts in GPU orchestration, from individual kernel drivers to federation across clusters. Powered by clusterdOS, our open-source distributed OS engine.
What's the difference between Aranya and clusterdOS?
clusterdOS is the open-source engine that lives inside each cluster and runs the core cluster-level applications. Aranya builds on top of those applications and runs both within and across your clusters, federating them so every cluster you run operates as one system.
Who is this built for?
On the demand-side, AI inference and training companies who cannot wait weeks for production clusters. Instead of working with datacenter hardware or generic managed Kubernetes, Aranya automates GPU orchestration, from kernel drivers to federation across clusters. On the supply-side, neoclouds and datacenters at 20MW and below who have the hardware but not the ability to turn it into compute for AI workloads. Aranya brings demand to your capacity without a platform team of your own.
We're a datacenter or neocloud. What does Aranya do for us?
If you have the hardware but not the platform layer to turn it into compute for AI workloads, Aranya closes that gap. We turn your bare metal into custom production-ready clusters in 48 hours and bring the demand side to your capacity. No platform team of your own, and no losing a customer because you can't match their specs. Built for neoclouds and datacenters at 20MW and below.
