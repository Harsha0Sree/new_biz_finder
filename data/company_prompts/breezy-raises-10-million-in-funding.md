About Breezy
Founded in 2024 and led by CEO James Harris, Breezy is an AI operating system purpose-built for residential real estate agents. The platform streamlines workflows, automates repetitive tasks, and delivers actionable insights. Breezy also includes Underbuilt, a proprietary data platform that reveals the true building potential of properties, empowering agents to generate insights and value for clients. Headquartered in Los Angeles, California, Breezy aims to redefine productivity for agents in a $500 billion global industry.

What Breezy Actually Does
What is Breezy?
Breezy is an AI-powered workspace built specifically for real estate agents. It runs the operational side of your business in one place—handling notes, follow-ups, comps, pipelines, and property insights—so you can spend less time on admin and more time closing deals.
What problems does Breezy solve for agents?
We built Breezy because agents can’t afford to be slowed down by clunky tech anymore. In an AI era where clients have more access to data, agents need a real edge—and it can’t come from juggling tools. It requires one connected system. That’s Breezy: one workspace that runs every part of an agent’s day. It creates branded comps, captures meeting notes, updates pipelines automatically, and surfaces build-potential insights no one else has—all in one simple, connected app that helps agents close more deals.
What makes Breezy different from other tools?
Breezy brings everything into one connected system—comps, notes, pipeline, and insights—so you don’t have to juggle multiple tools. Your data stays yours, independent of any brokerage, and Breezy gives you access to build-potential insights you won’t find anywhere else.
How is Breezy different from other AI platforms?
ChatGPT and other AI tools are general-purpose. Breezy is built in an agent’s brain. It connects to your deals, properties, and client activity—capturing conversations, organizing follow-ups, generating comps, and surfacing property insights automatically, all in one place.
How much does Breezy cost?
Breezy offers multiple plans designed to fit different agents and teams. You can view the full pricing details and available tiers directly in the app.
Is Breezy available in my area?
Breezy is expanding market by market. Comps depend on local MLS coverage, and UnderBuilt Radar currently supports over 1,200 cities—with thousands more being added.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Breezy offers multiple plans and payment options, as detailed in the app. You can cancel your subscription at any time, and your access will continue through the end of your current billing period. For full details, please refer to our Terms of Service.
