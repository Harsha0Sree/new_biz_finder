About Drafted
Founded in 2026 by CEO Nick Donahue, Drafted is a company that provides AI home design platform that enables users to create custom floor plans and 3D home layouts using natural language prompts. The company combines generative AI with residential design workflows to help homebuyers, architects, builders and developers rapidly explore and refine home concepts. Drafted’s platform is designed to make home design more accessible, affordable and interactive by allowing users to generate and modify architectural plans in real time.
