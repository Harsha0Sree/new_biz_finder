Seed Round
Axiomatic AI Raises $18 Million Seed Round
Axiomatic AI, a Cambridge, Massachusetts–based AI platform for verified science and engineering, has raised $18 million in Seed funding, bringing its total funding to $25 million.
Axiomatic AI, a Cambridge, Massachusetts–based AI platform for verified science and engineering, has raised $18 million in Seed funding, bringing its total funding to $25 million.
Investors
The round was led by Engine Ventures, with participation from Kleiner Perkins, Big Sur Ventures, Global Vision Capital, Propagator Ventures, and Liquid 2.
Engine Ventures is a venture capital firm focused on “tough tech” startups developing transformational science and engineering breakthroughs aimed at solving major global challenges. Its investment areas include energy and climate, human health, advanced systems, and infrastructure.
Axiomatic AI Use of Funds
The company plans to use the funding to expand enterprise deployments and deepen the integration of its verification platform into complex science and engineering workflows.
About Axiomatic AI
Founded by Dirk Englund, Frank Koppens, Joyce Poon, and Marin Soljačić, and led by CEO Jake Taylor, Axiomatic AI is building an engineering-focused AI platform designed to deliver interpretable, physics-grounded reasoning for complex hardware systems. Its core technology, Axiomatic Intelligence™, combines frontier AI models with formal mathematical and physics-based verification. Headquartered in Cambridge, Massachusetts, the company aims to become the authoritative workflow platform for science and engineering organizations operating in high-stakes domains.
