## Funding Article Content
Pre-seed Round
Causa Prima Raises $10M Pre-seed
Causa Prima raises $10M in pre-seed funding led by Creandum to build an AI-powered B2B payments network for automated invoice and dispute resolution.
Causa Prima Raises $10M Pre-seed
Causa Prima, based in Madrid and Munich, is a fintech startup that provides a shared network for B2B payments where AI agents for buyers and suppliers automatically handle invoices, disputes, and payment terms. The company has raised $10 million in pre-seed funding to advance its two-sided network platform.
Investors
The round was led by Creandum, a venture firm known for backing Klarna and Spotify. Additional participating investors include Kfund, HelloWorld, Angel Invest, and angel investors from Qonto, Pennylane, SAP, ING, SoFi, LIDL, and DeepMind.
Causa Prima Use of Funds
The new capital will be used to expand the company's engineering and product teams in Madrid and Munich as it works toward its goal of automating B2B payment cycles and eliminating traditional net payment terms.
About Causa Prima
Founded by Maex Ament, Henrik Gebbing, and Philip Stanislaus, Causa Prima provides a B2B payment network that connects buyers and suppliers. By utilizing AI agents, the platform facilitates real-time dispute resolution and invoicing, allowing companies to settle transactions instantly and secure early-payment discounts.
