Seed Round
F2 Raises $24 Million in Total Equity Capital
F2 raises $24M in total equity including a seed round led by HighlandX to scale its AI-driven operating system for private credit and commercial banking.
F2 Raises $24M in Total Equity Capital
F2, a New York-based compounding intelligence platform for private markets, has raised $24 million in total equity capital, including a $14 million seed round, to scale its agentic workflow platform for private credit funds and commercial banks.
Investors
The funding round was led by HighlandX, with participation from existing investors Left Lane Capital, NFX, Y Combinator, and Torch Capital.
F2 Use of Funds
The capital will be used to scale customized deployments of agentic workflows across private credit funds and commercial banks. The funding will also drive the expansion of the company's product suite, including its Institutional Knowledge platform and the new Portfolio Monitoring tool, alongside supporting the growth of engineering and enterprise deployment teams in New York, San Francisco, and London.
About F2
Led by CEO Don Muir, F2 is a company that provides a compounding intelligence platform for private markets.  The company offers an AI-driven system that transforms a firm's deal history into a queryable knowledge asset and automates credit underwriting and portfolio monitoring workflows. F2 serves customers including Bain Capital, Live Oak Bank, and dozens others across North America, the UK, Europe and Asia.
