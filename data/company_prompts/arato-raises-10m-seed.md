Seed Round
Arato Raises $10M Seed
Arato raises $10 million in a Seed round led by TLV Partners to develop its AI system testing and human behavior simulation platform.
Arato Raises $10M Seed
Arato, a Tel Aviv-based startup focused on AI testing and assurance, has secured $10 million in a Seed funding round. The company specializes in simulating user interactions to identify risks and performance issues in AI-driven products, providing a critical layer of quality control for enterprises before and after production deployment.
Investors
The funding round was led by TLV Partners. Additional investors included Jibe Ventures, Raghu Raghuram, and Marianna Tessel.
Arato Use of Funds
While specific details on the use of funds were not provided, the company plans to utilize the capital to continue the development of its AI testing platform and support its growth in the AI assurance market.
About Arato
Founded in 2024 by Shahar Erez, Hilik Paz, and Tal Salmona, Arato aims to bridge the gap between AI development and production-grade reliability. The company provides a platform that generates tens of thousands of scenarios to simulate human behavior across text, voice, and image interfaces, helping organizations measure the maturity and safety of their AI systems.
