Investors
The round was led by Staircase Ventures, a Toronto-based venture capital firm specializing in seed-stage investments. Founded in 2023, Staircase Ventures focuses on early-stage Canadian B2B technology companies.
Una Software Use of Funds
Proceeds used to deepen Una’s AI agent framework, expand its Global Template Library, and scale sales, marketing, and partnership efforts to meet growing midmarket and enterprise demand.
About Una Software
Founded in 2023, Una Software is an AI-powered FP&A platform designed for modern midmarket and enterprise organizations. Built as an AI-native, adaptive solution, Una enables finance teams to deliver intelligent forecasting, adaptive planning, and connected corporate performance management. Headquartered in Toronto, Canada, Una’s mission is to transform the finance function from a back-office requirement into a strategic engine for growth.
