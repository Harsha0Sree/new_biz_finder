Minimus Raises $51M Seed
Minimus, based in New York, is an enterprise cloud security company that specializes in vulnerability reduction and software supply chain protection. The company announced it has raised $51 million in a seed funding round to support the growth and development of its security platform.
Investors
This funding round was backed by YL Ventures and Mayfield.
Minimus Use of Funds
Minimus plans to use the capital to support the continued development of its cloud vulnerability reduction platform, including its recently launched supply chain protection and developer tool suites, as well as to scale its operations.
About Minimus
Founded in 2022 by industry veterans behind Twistlock and the NIST SP 800-190 standard, Minimus focuses on neutralizing cloud infrastructure vulnerabilities at the source. The company provides tools that optimize developer workflows and simplify risk management for enterprises.
