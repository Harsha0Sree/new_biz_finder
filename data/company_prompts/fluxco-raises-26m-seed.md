Fluxco raises $26M seed round led by Congruent Ventures and 8VC to expand its software platform for the electrical transformer supply chain.
Fluxco Raises $26M Seed
Fluxco, an Austin-based software developer, has raised $26 million in seed funding. The company specializes in building software platforms to streamline the procurement process for electrical transformers.
Investors
This round was led by Congruent Ventures and 8VC. Additional participating investors included Cubit Capital, White Star Capital, Trust Ventures, Overture VC, and New System Ventures.
Fluxco Use of Funds
The company plans to use the capital to support the growth of its software platform and scale its operations in the electrical transformer procurement market.
About Fluxco
Fluxco is an Austin-based software developer that provides a digital platform for finding and buying electrical transformers, helping to modernize the supply chain and procurement process for energy infrastructure components.
