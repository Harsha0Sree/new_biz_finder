Hang Ten Systems Raises $32M Seed
Hang Ten Systems, an enterprise AI services company based in Palo Alto, California, has raised $32 million in seed funding. The capital will support the company as it builds its team and expands its operations helping global enterprises implement AI.
Investors
The round was led by Mayfield, with a strategic investment from Aramco Ventures and participation from a group of angel investors.
Hang Ten Systems Use of Funds
Hang Ten Systems plans to use the funding to grow its team and scale its work with global enterprises by delivering AI-native project solutions.
About Hang Ten Systems
Hang Ten Systems was founded by Dr. Vishal Sikka, the former CEO of Infosys and Executive Board Member at SAP. The company provides an AI-native operating model that allows large enterprises to build, change, and run business software using agentic code generation and a reusable skills library to lower costs and accelerate timelines.

02 / Platform
Hobie
Hobie
is our agentic AI platform. Five layers read your live
          systems, model your business, and answer questions that trace back to the source data.
One platform, five layers
Shoreline
Customer-facing surface
The conversational surface people actually use: chat with memory, clarification, and intent routing, in the browser or piped into tools like Claude and ChatGPT Desktop.
Shaper
Builds & refines the agents
Builds each agent by reading your live systems, then keeps improving it on a closed loop: evaluate, diagnose, fix, verify. Most of the work is deterministic compilation, so results are repeatable.
Lens
The semantic layer
A published, versioned model of your business: datasets, metrics, relationships, and a plain-language vocabulary. Business logic lives here, editable without touching database views.
Reef
Knowledge, queried in place
Runs queries in place against your existing systems, with nothing copied or migrated. One shared model maps many of them onto the same concepts.
Beacon
Audit & governance
Spans every layer. Each answer traces to the exact data and model version behind it, with fail-closed, role-aware access and per-caller credentials.
Requests flow down through the layers. Grounded, governed answers flow back up, and Beacon records every step.
