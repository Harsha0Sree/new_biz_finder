Seed Round
Archestra.AI Raises $10M Seed
Archestra.AI raises $10M seed round led by 20VC to scale its secure open-source platform for enterprise AI agent data integration.
Archestra.AI Raises $10M Seed
Archestra.AI, a London-based startup providing an open-source platform for secure enterprise AI agent deployment, has raised $10 million in a seed funding round. This latest investment brings the company total funding to $13.5 million to date.
Investors
The round was led by 20VC, with participation from Visible Ventures and Tenacity Capital. Additional investors include angel investors Olivier Pomel, CEO and co-founder of Datadog, and Kieran Flanagan, CMO of Hubspot.
Archestra.AI Use of Funds
The company plans to use the new capital to expand its go-to-market and engineering teams and to accelerate enterprise deployments of its platform.
About Archestra.AI
Founded in 2025 by alumni from Grafana Labs, Archestra.AI provides an open-source platform that enables enterprises to connect sensitive internal data to AI agents securely. The company offers guardrails to prevent data leaks and hallucinations, allowing businesses to integrate agents with legal and HR data without relying solely on large model providers.
