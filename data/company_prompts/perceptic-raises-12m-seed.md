Perceptic
, a London, UK-based provider of developer of an end-to-end AI platform for drug development, raised $12m in seed funding.
The round was led by Accel, with participation from Air Street Capital and Elder Gull.
The company intends to use the funds to scale its engineering capabilities, expand its customer base, and grow its presence within the United States market.
Led by Co-Founders Tilman Flock (CEO), Martin Copes, and Zaki Trache, Perceptic develops an infrastructure-agnostic operating layer that unifies siloed pharmaceutical R&D workflows, automating scientific due diligence, clinical trial indication selection, and data harmonization through traceable AI agents.
The company serves multiple top-tier pharmaceutical corporations, including CSL, and said it has delivered a 50-fold increase in clinical data extractions during paid production deployments.

Mission
Drug development is one of humanity's highest-stakes forms of reasoning. Every decision carries years of work, billions in capital, and the possibility of changing patients' lives.
But the systems supporting those decisions were not built for the complexity of modern science.
The future will not be one model making one answer. It will be teams of scientists working with intelligent systems that remember, connect, challenge, and explain.
We believe every insight should compound. Every claim should have provenance. Every team should inherit the full context of the work that came before. And every drug should be followed as one continuous story, not passed between disconnected departments.
