About VideoTutor
VideoTutor is an AI-powered education technology company focused on transforming how students learn by generating real-time instructional videos. The company provides an AI tutoring platform that generates real-time visual instructional videos in response to student questions. Founded by Gen Z entrepreneurs, VideoTutor automates the entire pipeline, from parsing a student query to structuring pedagogy, generating visuals, and delivering narrated instruction in real time.
