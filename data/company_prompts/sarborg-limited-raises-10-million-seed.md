Sarborg Limited Raises $10 Million Seed
Sarborg Limited, based in Wilmington, Delaware, a company specializing in AI-driven agentic software for drug repurposing and decision optimization, has successfully raised $10 million in a Seed funding round. This latest investment brings the company's total funding to $10 million to support its proprietary technology and expand its operational capabilities.
Investors
This funding round was led by Corvus Capital.
Sarborg Limited Use of Funds
The company plans to use the new capital to advance its proprietary AI agents focused on drug repurposing, target identification, and clinical trial optimization. Additionally, funds will be allocated to develop new pharmaceutical intellectual property, grow the internal pipeline of pharmaceutical assets for monetization, and expand the company's client base and revenue-generating service business.
About Sarborg Limited
Founded in 2024, Sarborg Limited integrates proprietary AI agents, mathematical algorithms, and cybernetics to optimize decision-making processes. The company primarily utilizes machine learning and algorithmic strategies to evaluate drug repurposing by matching drug and disease signatures, aiming to improve the probability of success in clinical trials. The company is currently scaling its technology for applications beyond the pharmaceutical sector.

ABOUT SARBORG
We believe signatures are
the universal language of systems.
Sarborg Limited is an agentic AI signature intelligence business. We build autonomous agents that identify, interpret, and generate high-value opportunities across multiple sectors - from human therapeutics to agricultural chemistry.
Our core thesis is simple: every biological, chemical, and industrial system emits a signature. By encoding those signatures into a common language, we make them comparable, learnable, and exploitable by an agentic network that compounds its intelligence with every match.
The result is a continuously evolving discovery engine - one that can move a question from raw input to ranked, mechanistically-explained output in less time than it takes to read a paper.
