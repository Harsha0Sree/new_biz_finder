Jimini Health Raises $17M Seed Round
Jimini Health, a New York–based company building clinician-supervised, patient-facing AI infrastructure for large behavioral health provider organizations, has raised $17 million in Seed funding.
Jimini Health, a New York–based company building clinician-supervised, patient-facing AI infrastructure for large behavioral health provider organizations, has raised $17 million in Seed funding.
Investors
The round was led by M13, with participation from Town Hall Ventures, LionBird, Zetta Venture Partners, and OneMind, bringing Jimini Health’s total funding to over $25 million.
M13 is an early-stage venture capital firm based in Santa Monica and New York. Founded in 2016, the firm focuses on seed and Series A investments in disruptive software businesses. M13 combines funding with a venture studio model, offering strategic guidance in areas such as product development, marketing, and customer acquisition.
Jimini Health Use of Funds
The company will use the funding to expand AI development across additional clinical settings and scale partnerships with large behavioral health providers nationwide.
About Jimini Health
Led by CEO Luis Voloch, Jimini Health is a healthcare technology company building clinician-supervised AI infrastructure for large behavioral health provider organizations. The platform enables providers to deploy AI safely, compliantly, and at scale, with licensed clinicians maintaining oversight of every patient interaction. Its core platform, Sage, supports the implementation of AI in clinical environments while adhering to regulatory requirements. Designed for regulated healthcare settings, Sage integrates directly with clinical organizations, enabling the industry to adopt AI effectively within existing care models.

WHAT IT TAKES
Science & Technology
Technologies like mRNA, complex antibodies, and cell therapies have improved nearly every aspect of human health in the last 30 years — but therapy and its clinical outcomes have not changed much. Jimini has built a scientifically rigorous, consumer-grade, LLM-augmented experience that is a radical improvement over traditional therapy.
Safe, Clinically Integrated AI
AI and LLMs will impact every aspect of healthcare, including therapy. There are real risks if these technologies are developed without adequate safeguards or clinical integration. In response, Jimini has developed a narrow, specific, and well-validated approach to its AI development. Sage is not a general-purpose chatbot but rather supports the therapist and patient with specialized evidence-based approaches.
Clinically Rigorous, Clinician-Led
World leaders in psychology designed Jimini’s care model to ensure that every part of the patient's journey is clinically beneficial. Jimini does not believe that LLMs should replace clinicians; rather, AI will help therapists deliver higher-quality care and focus on truly the most complex parts of their job.

Sage, the always-accessible AI behavioral health assistant, works with providers and patients as part of the care team. Sage is never a substitute for human clinicians.
Life happens when patients are not in session with their provider. Sage is there every day: before, after, and between sessions. Whether the patient needs support and reminders, needs to vent, or wants to practice a new skill they learned in session, Sage is here for them.
Developed by a world-leading team of psychologists, psychiatrists, and AI experts, with clinical rigor and safety at the forefront.
Sage both receives instruction from the clinical team to best support the specific patient needs, and also escalates necessary situations to them. Sage shares patient progress with the provider so they can best understand and care for patients.
