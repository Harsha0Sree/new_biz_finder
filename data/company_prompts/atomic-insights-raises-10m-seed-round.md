About Atomic Insights
Founded in 2023, Atomic Insights is a financial technology company that provides money-movement and workflow automation tools for registered investment advisors (RIAs) and family offices. The platform automates client payment workflows end-to-end, capturing payment requests, orchestrating approvals, and executing payments via real-time custodian and bank APIs, while providing a complete audit trail and cashflow reporting in a single, centralized workspace. Atomic Insights integrates bi-directionally with leading CRM and portfolio management systems.

Atomic Insights Launches Schwab Money Movement API
Lucas Babbitt
June 24, 2026
I'm thrilled to share that Atomic Insights is the first fintech to go live on Schwab Advisor Services' new Move Money Transfers API. Starting today, ACH payments transmit directly from the Atomic Platform into Schwab. No portal re-entry, no manual hand-offs.
When we started Atomic, the problem we kept seeing was that wealth management had digitized the surface layer, but the operational engine underneath stayed manual and fragmented. Ops teams were still re-keying payment details into custodian portals to get transactions done.
This is the fix. With direct API execution, payments move from instruction to settlement inside one unified workflow - ingestion, validation, approval, transmission, and reconciliation, all in one place. ACH is the first of four payment types rolling out through Schwab's framework; wires, journals, and checks will follow.
It's a meaningful step toward the model we've always believed in: execution living inside the system, not bolted on top of it. And it's the first phase of a deeper integration with Schwab's money movement infrastructure, extending end-to-end execution across custodians and payment types.
We're grateful to the Schwab Advisor Services team for building this alongside us, and to the RIAs and Family Offices, now representing over $120 billion in assets on platform, who've trusted us to modernize how they move money.
