Nace.AI Raises $21.5M Seed Round
Nace.AI, a Palo Alto, California-based applied AI company building specialized intelligence powered by its MetaModel architecture, has raised $21.5 million in Seed funding.
Nace.AI, a Palo Alto, California-based applied AI company building specialized intelligence powered by its MetaModel architecture, has raised $21.5 million in Seed funding.
Alongside the funding round, the company is launching its new product in research preview: a model for professional work in which more than 100 specialized AI agents autonomously manage complex workflows.
Investors
The round was led by Walden Catalyst, with participation from General Catalyst and several institutional and angel investors.
Walden Catalyst is a San Francisco-based venture capital firm focused on early-stage deep tech investments. The firm operates internationally, backing founders across the U.S., Europe, and Israel. With an inaugural fund of $550 million, Walden Catalyst supports companies in the AI, semiconductor, cloud infrastructure, and digital biology sectors.
Nace.AI Use of Funds
The company will use the funding to expand Nace.AI’s research preview platform, further develop its MetaModel technology and specialized AI agents, and grow its expert validation network for enterprise workflows.
About Nace.AI
Led by CEO Dos Baha, Nace.AI is an applied AI company building a specialized intelligence powered by Metamodel designed to run companies. The company combines proprietary small language models, reinforcement learning agents, and human expert validation to deliver explainable, enterprise outputs for complex knowledge work. Headquartered in Palo Alto, California, Nace AI’s long-term vision is to build a specialized intelligence that runs companies while redefining the role of knowledge workers.

Nace.AI was born from our collective experiences at Google, Meta, Amazon, and the University of Toronto. Headquartered in Palo Alto, California.
Where we began
When we came together in the summer of 2024 to build Nace.AI, we shared a singular vision inspired by a challenge we'd encountered time and again: the difficulty enterprises face in adapting mainstream language models to meet their specific business needs. As machine learning researchers and engineers with years of experience, we knew this wasn't just a technical and research hurdle — it was an AI trustworthiness problem.
We heard stories from enterprises about compliance headaches, factual inaccuracies, operational risks, and contextual misunderstandings that left organizations vulnerable. While undeniably powerful, generic language models weren't enough to address these challenges. Enterprises needed solutions that would not only work but instill confidence. That's where our journey began.

Nace.AI raises $21.5M to build AI-led enterprise workflows
Nace.AI
Nace.AI has raised $21.5 million in seed funding, led by Walden Catalyst with participation from General Catalyst and other institutional and angel investors.
This funding will accelerate our work on specialized intelligence for complex enterprise workflows. We are building systems that work across financial histories, operational data, legal documents, and other unstructured files to produce precise, decision-ready outputs.
Building intelligence for the enterprise
At the core is MetaModel, our proprietary technology for turning enterprise policies and procedures into small language models tailored to real-world business environments. These models learn from company-specific workflows and adapt to evolving policies, standards, and knowledge.
“Our AI agents continuously learn and adapt to company-specific environments, powered by our MetaModel architecture. We execute work at scale, while experts provide final judgment and accountability.”
— Dos Baha, Founder and CEO of Nace.AI
We will continue advancing MetaModel, expanding our family of specialized models, and building end-to-end systems for finance, audit, and compliance. Our focus is on personalization, continuous knowledge updates, reliable execution, and explainability.
This investment brings us closer to our long-term vision: specialized intelligence that can run the complex workflows at the heart of a company, with experts providing final validation.
