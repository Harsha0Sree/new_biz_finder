Seed Round
Nullify Secures $12.5M Seed Round

Nullify, a San Francisco, CA–based AI workforce platform for product security, has secured a $12.5 million Seed funding round.
Investors
The round was led by SYN Ventures, with participation from existing investor Black Nova Venture Capital.
SYN Ventures is a venture capital firm specializing in early-stage investments in cybersecurity, industrial security, national defense, and data privacy. The firm manages over $600 million across multiple funds and supports founders with capital, operational scaling, and go-to-market strategies tailored to security-focused innovation.
Nullify Use of Funds
The company will use the funding to scale go-to-market operations, expand engineering and research teams, and drive global growth.
About Nullify
Led by CEO Shan Kulkarni, Nullify is the creator of an AI workforce for product security, delivering autonomous AI employees that handle vulnerability detection, triage, validation, and remediation end-to-end. By ingesting code, cloud configurations, and business context, Nullify autonomously generates and validates exploits, prioritizes real business risk, and delivers merge-ready fixes without manual intervention. Headquartered in San Francisco, CA, with offices in Sydney, Australia, Nullify serves mid-sized enterprises and high-growth SaaS companies worldwide.
