MOTHER.tech Raises $15M Seed
MOTHER.tech, a creative studio based in Brooklyn, has raised $15 million in seed funding. The company uses a multi-model AI render pipeline to power modular creative tools called "gens" and has launched a one-tap AI creative app called Degen.
Investors
GV led the funding round. Additional participating investors included Lerer Hippeau Ventures, BoxGroup, and Shine Capital.
MOTHER.tech Use of Funds
The company plans to use the funding to launch its Degen app, which is currently available on iOS and Android by invite code.
About MOTHER.tech
Founded in 2023 by Kelsey Falter and Raissa Chagas, MOTHER.tech's mission is to provide an AI render pipeline that powers modular creative tools, or "gens." Artists, photographers, or designers can turn their visual style into a one-tap tool for others to use. Users can select a generator, add their own photos, references, or text, and receive publish-ready results rapidly. The Degen app emphasizes speed, specificity, and the participatory side of internet culture, allowing users to remix and combine generators and share content privately.
