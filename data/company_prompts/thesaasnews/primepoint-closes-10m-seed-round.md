Seed Round
Primepoint Closes $10M Seed Round

Primepoint, a San Mateo, CA–based construction intelligence platform, has closed $10 million in Seed funding.
Investors
The round was led by Navitas Capital, with participation from Penny Jar Capital, NextView Ventures, GS Futures, Aglaé Ventures, and angel investor Dr. Yann LeCun.
Navitas Capital is a U.S.-based venture capital firm that focuses on investing in technology companies transforming the built environment, primarily real estate, construction, and related industries. Founded in 2009 and headquartered in Culver City, California, the firm invests in early-to-growth-stage startups.
Primepoint Use of Funds
The company will use the funds to expand its platform capabilities and grow its customer base among large commercial contractors in the United States.
About Primepoint
Led by CEO Lubomir Bourdev, Primepoint is a construction intelligence platform designed to read, connect, and act on construction drawings. Powered by proprietary AI with deep construction expertise, the platform automates daily workflows, including constructability review, RFI drafting, submittal analysis, and drawing navigation, delivering precise and traceable results grounded in project documents. Founded in 2024 and headquartered in San Mateo, California, Primepoint serves large commercial general contractors.

Data practices
Built on a foundation of data isolation and encryption
Single-tenant isolation
Each project is provisioned as its own tenant container with separate data stores. Tenants do not share databases, indices, or file buckets, preventing cross-customer data paths.
Always-on encryption
All customer data is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard cryptography. BYOK is available for customers requiring stricter key governance.
No training on your data
Your data serves your organization only. We do not use customer data to train foundation models or build derivative LLMs shared across customers.
Secure identity management built for enterprise teams
Enterprise SSO integration
Integrate with your identity provider using SAML 2.0 or OIDC for Okta, Microsoft Entra ID, Google, and other providers with automated user provisioning.
Role-based access control
Built-in RBAC restricts access by project and role, with SCIM support for automated user lifecycle management including provisioning and de-provisioning.
Minimal permission scopes
Integrations request only the specific permissions required for functionality. Access tokens can be revoked at any time by your administrators.
