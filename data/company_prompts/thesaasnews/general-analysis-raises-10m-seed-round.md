Seed Round
General Analysis Raises $10M Seed Round
General Analysis, a San Francisco, CA–based company building security infrastructure for agentic AI, has raised $10 million in Seed funding.
General Analysis, a San Francisco, CA–based company building security infrastructure for agentic AI, has raised $10 million in Seed funding.
Investors
The round was led by Altos Ventures, with participation from 645 Ventures, Menlo Ventures, Y Combinator, as well as additional strategic investors and angel backers.
Founded in 1996, Altos Ventures is a Silicon Valley–based technology investment firm. Through patient and pragmatic investing, Altos partners with early- to growth-stage technology companies across both consumer and enterprise sectors, aiming to build durable, compounding businesses over decades. As a registered investment advisor (RIA) with the SEC, Altos employs a flexible, long-term, and concentrated approach to venture capital, supporting companies throughout their lifecycle, from inception to global growth and profitability.
General Analysis Use of Funds
The company will use the funding to advance its security platform, expand its adversarial testing capabilities, and support enterprise adoption of safer agentic AI systems.
About General Analysis
General Analysis is an AI security company that helps enterprises deploy agentic AI safely through adversarial evaluation and defensive tooling. Founded in 2025 and based in San Francisco, the company focuses on identifying and mitigating risks in autonomous AI agents operating in complex, real-world environments. Its platform simulates adversarial scenarios to expose vulnerabilities, such as prompt injection and data leakage and provides enterprises with actionable insights and defensive configurations to reduce risk.
