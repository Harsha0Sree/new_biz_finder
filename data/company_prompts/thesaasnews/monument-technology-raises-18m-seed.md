Monument Technology
, the technology unit of UK challenger
Monument Bank
, has raised more than £18 million in seed funding, almost doubling its initial £10 million target.
Monument Technology was spun out of its parent company in 2023, when the bank looked to make its infrastructure available for other institutions through a Banking-Platform-as-a-Service (BPaaS) offering.
The subsidiary provides a white-label banking platform that enables financial institutions to offer deposits, savings, lending, and mortgage services to their customers. The platform integrates with third-party providers, like Mambu and Salesforce, combining payments, banking infrastructure, and customer channels into a single solution.
With the new funds, Monument Technology states it plans to scale the business domestically and internationally, accelerate implementation timelines, and develop its platform. This includes launching new payments and current account solutions, which are "coming soon", according to the firm's website.
