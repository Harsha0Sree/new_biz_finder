About Grotto AI
Founded by Nick Deveau and Ben Epstein, Grotto AI is a vertical AI company that helps multifamily operators reduce vacancy loss by supercharging their leasing teams. It delivers real-time guidance and post-call coaching that turns leasing conversations into measurable revenue. The company leverages advanced machine learning models trained on hundreds of thousands of leasing interactions to identify behavioral signals that drive lease conversions. Headquartered in New York, Grotto AI's platform currentl use by major operators such as Weidner Apartment Homes. Grotto's mission is to help humans shine in the moments that matter most.
