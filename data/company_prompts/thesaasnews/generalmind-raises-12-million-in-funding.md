About Bofrost
bofrost* is Europe's market leader in the direct distribution of frozen food and ice cream specialties. Founded in 1966 and headquartered in Straelen, Germany, the family-owned company supplies around 3.7 million customer households through roughly 250 branches in 11 European countries and generated about €1.43bn in revenue in FY 2024/25 with more than 11,000 employees.
The heart of the business is a fleet of around 5,900 special-purpose sales vehicles with refrigerated and freezer bodies that bring frozen products directly to customers' doors. Keeping this fleet on the road is business-critical, which makes fleet management a demanding, high-volume operational function.
Use-case definition
The focus is
Accounts Payable invoice processing for fleet management
: handling workshop, tire, and fuel invoices that arrive in varying formats and levels of detail. GeneralMind reads and structures them, allocates every position to the correct booking account, and runs automated plausibility checks that previously required deep expert knowledge.
The challenge
The fleet management department is responsible for maintaining and operating the vehicle fleet. A core part of this work is invoice verification: workshop invoices, tire invoices, and fuel invoices were captured and checked manually and then allocated to the individual booking accounts. This required very deep domain expertise, since the correct account depends on what was repaired on a vehicle and how.
Several pressures compound the problem:
Rising volume, shrinking team
Invoice volumes keep climbing while the team keeps getting smaller, tightening capacity every year.
Expertise walking out
Several team members retire soon, risking the loss of deep, specialised knowledge held by a few.
Complex workshop invoices
Highly detailed, heterogeneous technical invoices make manual capture slow and error-prone.
A business-critical process rests on a handful of experts and manual steps — slow to run, easy to break, and impossible to scale.
The solution
Together with GeneralMind, bofrost* has automated large parts of this process, with several steps running fully autonomously. Technical invoices, above all the detailed workshop invoices, are now captured cleanly with all their line-item depth and posted to the correct booking accounts depending on what was repaired on the vehicle and how.
On top of the pure capture and posting, the system runs automated plausibility checks, for example:
Duplicate detection
flagging repairs that should not have been invoiced because the same component was already repaired shortly before.
Content plausibility
checking whether invoice positions are consistent with the vehicle and the reported repair.
Employees stay in the loop where it matters: they review proposed bookings where needed, while confident cases run through automatically. All automated actions remain fully visible for later review, preserving transparency and an audit trail.
Expert knowledge now lives in the system: invoices are captured, posted, and checked automatically, with people reviewing only what matters.

About Kloeckner metals
Kloeckner & Co is one of the world’s leading producer-independent distributors of steel and metal products, founded in 1906 and headquartered in Düsseldorf, Germany. Notably, Kloeckner is widely recognized as a pioneer of digital transformation in the traditionally analog steel industry, repositioning itself from a pure distributor into a data-driven, platform-based business. About Oatly
Oatly is the world’s original and largest oat drink company. Founded in Sweden on the basis of research at Lund University and headquartered in Malmö, Oatly offers a broad portfolio of dairy alternatives, from oat drinks to ice cream, yogurt, and cooking creams. The brand is available in more than 50 countries and generated roughly $860m in revenue in 2025 with around 1,400 employees.
Oatly grew extremely fast from a challenger brand into the global category leader. As the company matures, profitability has moved into focus: improving margins is a strategic priority, which puts operational efficiency across the order-to-cash process center stage.
Use-case definition
The focus is
Sales Order Processing
: reading, structuring, and completeness-checking of orders that arrive through manual channels in multiple languages and formats, followed by booking into the ERP. GeneralMind handles this variability reliably and escalates open questions instead of letting incomplete orders slip through.
The challenge
A substantial share of Oatly’s incoming order volume, more than half, does not arrive through structured channels such as EDI or automated interfaces, but through manual channels, above all email inboxes.
Before GeneralMind, employees speaking various languages handled these orders entirely by hand:
Receiving each order and checking it for completeness.
Raising queries with the customer whenever information was missing.
Transferring the completed order into the ERP for further processing and dispatch.
This manual, repetitive work is slow and costly at scale, and it sits in direct tension with the company’s margin targets.
The solution
GeneralMind was deployed exactly at this interface between unstructured inbound orders and the ERP. The solution drastically accelerates the process and automates it with the
Autopilot
, so that order capture now runs fully automatically and in some cases autonomously. The system is especially powerful because it can fuzzily match and structure SOs from incomplete information through an AI-Fuzzy-Match.
Fuzzy match
GeneralMind handles unstructured and incomplete information through fuzzy match against master-data, saving operators tremendous time.
Human in the loop
Where needed, staff review proposed orders and can adjust, correct, or refine them quickly and intuitively.
Confidence score
Where the AI is confident, the Autopilot executes the action autonomously.
Expert judgment stays with the team, while the Autopilot handles order capture — running partly, and often fully, automatically.

Accuracy & Learning
What accuracy can we expect from day one?
+
The solution is pre-trained on a representative sample of your own data before go-live, so it arrives calibrated rather than cold. Clients typically see ~85% straight-through accuracy from day one, rising to 93–95% for the majority of transactions within four weeks as the context layer refines on real interactions and operator feedback.
What is the roadmap to full automation?
+
We deliver ~85% accuracy from day one, rising continuously as the system learns from operator feedback and real transaction data. Clients typically start with human review across all cases, then progressively move individual transactions onto autopilot as their confidence score crosses the recommended threshold of 93–95%. Within four weeks, 80–90% of cases reach that threshold — meaning the vast majority of your volume can be running on full autopilot within a month.
How does the model learn and improve over time?
+
Every operator action — corrections, approvals, escalations — is captured as a "decision trace" and fed back into the context layer. This continuously sharpens the system's accuracy for your specific data patterns, supplier behaviour, and business rules, without requiring manual retraining or any IT involvement.
How is the confidence score calculated?
+
It draws on a proprietary combination of extraction certainty, contextual match quality, rule-compliance signals, and historical per-sender accuracy — calibrated specifically to each use case rather than applied as a generic threshold. This means items are only surfaced for human review when the system has genuine uncertainty, making the human-in-the-loop handoff reliable rather than arbitrary.
Can the solution handle large or complex documents?
+
Yes, without limitation. Large documents are decomposed into parallel processing units, so a 100-page PDF or a multi-tab Excel file is handled with the same throughput and accuracy as a single page. Non-standard layouts, mixed languages, and complex formatting are supported natively.
Integration & Technical
How does the technical integration with our systems work?
+
We integrate with all major ERP systems — SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, and others. Rather than prescribing a fixed approach, we define the integration strategy together with your IT team upfront, selecting the right connectivity method (middleware, OData/REST, IDoc/EDI, or direct APIs) based on your existing landscape and governance requirements. Our forward-deployment team then owns the full implementation. From your side, we need an initial architecture alignment session, relevant system access, and security approvals — typically 1–3 hours per week during setup only.
Can escalation paths and downstream workflow steps also be automated?
+
Yes, fully. When an exception is detected — for example, a price discrepancy between a PO and its confirmation — the system can route it automatically to the responsible team based on your defined escalation logic. Once resolved, the feedback is captured and downstream steps resume automatically: no manual re-entry required.
How many article numbers or SKUs can the solution handle?
+
There is no practical upper limit. Active deployments range from a few hundred to several hundred thousand article numbers within a single use case, with no impact on processing speed or accuracy at scale.
Partnership & Support
