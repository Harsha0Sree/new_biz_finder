Knit Health Use of Funds
The company will use the funding to accelerate the development and deployment of its Large Clinical Behavior Model (LCBM) across healthcare systems to improve clinical operations and patient care.
About Knit Health
Founded in 2025, Knit Health is a healthcare technology company building AI that captures collective clinical intelligence to serve as infrastructure for better healthcare. Spun out of the University of California, Berkeley, the company leverages its proprietary Large Clinical Behavior Model (LCBM) to analyze EMR data from 30 U.S. health systems and optimize workflows such as triage, patient flow, referrals, and care coordination. Headquartered in San Francisco, California, Knit Health is built with full HIPAA compliance and is currently partnering with healthcare systems on initial deployments focused on operational efficiency and care delivery optimization.

A world model for how healthcare actually works
The Healthcare AI Infrastructure Layer
Knit is a world model purpose-built for healthcare. This is the next frontier in AI that is trained not on language like LLMs, but on the real actions of real clinicians treating real patients. It learns how care actually unfolds: the full space of possible patient pathways, and the real probabilities of what happens next.
The Knit platform is a unified intelligence layer, trained on hundreds of millions of clinical actions and EHR data. It replaces a patchwork of point solutions with one model that understands the whole system.
