Frequently asked questions
What is a LemonSlice avatar?
LemonSlice avatars are interactive video avatars that engage in real-time conversations. They are a natural extension of voice agents. You can add one to your website or use the LemonSlice
API
to build avatars into your products. LemonSlice offers the best video avatars in the world - and the only ones that work for both photorealistic and cartoon characters.
Do avatars respond in real time?
Yes. LemonSlice avatars are designed to reply instantly, creating natural, back-and-forth interactions just like a real conversation.
Do LemonSlice avatars support different languages?
Yes, LemonSlice avatars can speak in any language, allowing you to create engaging experiences for global audiences.
Can I use my own large language model (LLM) or AI Voice model?
Yes. Our

allows you to create a real-time avatar from any LLM or AI Voice model.
