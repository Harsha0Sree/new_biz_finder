Investors
The funding round was led by Expeditions. Additional participants included Theon, various American and European venture capital firms, family offices, and individual angel investors with backgrounds at Palantir, Anduril, and Quorum.
Twin Prime Use of Funds
The capital will be used to develop and refine the company's proprietary frontier AI models designed for high-stakes defence and security environments, specifically focusing on sensor data fusion and real-time decision-making.
About Twin Prime
Founded in 2025 by George Lentzas, Stephane Sezer, Drew Calcagno, and Michael Leite-Garcia, Twin Prime is a new frontier AI lab, dedicated to defense and security. The company is developing AI models that natively reason on data from the large number of sensor modalities in the physical world, notably across the defense and security landscape, to enable operators to act urgently and effectively.
