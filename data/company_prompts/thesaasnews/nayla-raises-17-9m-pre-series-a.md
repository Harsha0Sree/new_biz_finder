Saudi fintech startup Nayla raises $17.9M in a Pre-Series A round led by Idrisi Ventures to expand its AI-powered digital financing platform for businesses.
Nayla Raises $17.9M Pre-Series A
Nayla, a Saudi fintech startup based in Riyadh, has successfully raised $17.9 million in a Pre-Series A funding round consisting of both equity and debt financing. The company provides digital financing solutions for micro-enterprises across the Saudi market.
Investors
The funding round was led by Idrisi Ventures, with participation from Suhail Ventures and several strategic investors. Blominvest led the credit facility portion of the transaction.
Nayla Use of Funds
Nayla intends to utilize the new capital to scale its financing operations for micro-enterprises throughout Saudi Arabia. The company also plans to expand into new customer segments and enhance its AI-powered credit assessment platform.
About Nayla
Established in 2025 in partnership with Sanabil Studio, the venture-building arm of Sanabil Investments, Nayla provides digital financing solutions for micro-enterprises. The company leverages alternative data and advanced credit assessment models to serve businesses that may not rely on traditional collateral or financial statements.
