Luel Use of Funds
The capital will be used to scale the company's AI data training marketplace and expand operations as it seeks to meet the growing demand from AI labs for verified human-generated data.
About Luel
Founded in 2026 by William Namgyal and Inigo Lenderking, Luel is an AI data marketplace that connects individuals with paid opportunities to generate training data for artificial intelligence models. The platform enables AI companies to acquire consent-based datasets through a global network of contributors who complete tasks such as image collection, video recording, transcription, and voice capture.
