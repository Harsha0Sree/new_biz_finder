Twin1 AI
, a San Mateo, CA-based provider of an AI-powered digital twin platform for knowledge workers, raised $20M in Seed funding.
The round was co-led by Bessemer Venture Partners, Tribeca Venture Partners, and Aramco Ventures, with participation from EJF Ventures, Tin Alley Ventures, AGI House Ventures, Neo, F-Prime, Btech Consortium, Antiportfolio Ventures, Lakestar, Notion Capital, Insiders, Orrick, Haakon Overli, Roy Reznick, Hans Tung, Kevin Buehler, Robert Trezona, and Dan Wong.
The company intends to use the funds to expand its teams in San Mateo, CA and London, UK, invest in go-to-market efforts, and further develop its core technology.
Led by CEO Dr. Lewis Z. Liu and co-founders Tom Cahn, Huiting Liu, and Dr. Jonathan Budd, Twin1 AI is a provider of a a coordination and trust layer service for enterprise AI that pairs professionals with AI-powered digital twins to scale their expertise.
Their platform features continuously evolving digital twins grounded in user work context, six layers of privacy and governance controls, a peer-to-peer Twin Network, an enterprise MCP server, and AI-mediated communications operating across tools like Slack, Microsoft Teams, Outlook, Gmail, Google Drive, and SharePoint.
