Technical context
geoSurge's product combines visibility monitoring with a trademarked methodology it calls Corpus Engineering, which the company says spans both what generative systems retrieve in real time and what they have already learned and retained internally about a brand. CEO Francisco Vigo framed the distinction directly: "A lot of the market is still thinking about AI visibility like SEO and citation tracking... the real battleground is how models learn, understand, remember and represent brands over time," he said in the company's release. Dealroom, an independent deals database, notes the round ranks in the 99th percentile by size among UK seed deals in its sector, while also cautioning that geoSurge is betting on an "unproven" deeper layer, influencing how models represent entities, in a category that, in Dealroom's words, "barely exists yet."
For practitioners
The pitch maps onto real technical work: dataset curation and provenance tagging, fine-tuning or retrieval strategies that shape entity representations, and evaluation harnesses that measure representation fidelity rather than just citation frequency. Teams building or auditing RAG pipelines, brand-safety tooling, or LLM evaluation frameworks may find the underlying problem, how consistently and accurately a model represents a given entity, familiar even though "Corpus Engineering" is unverified vendor terminology rather than a peer-reviewed technique.
What to watch
Because the category and the methodology are both new and single-vendor-described, watch for independent benchmarks of representation fidelity, case studies with measurable before/after changes in model outputs, and whether enterprise procurement starts treating AI representation as a defined, auditable requirement rather than a marketing claim.

What identifies us
geoSurge is an AI-applied deep-tech company helping brands shape how they are represented inside generative AI systems.
As AI becomes a primary way people discover information, visibility is changing. Users are no longer presented with a list of blue links. They are given answers. Those answers impact which brands are seen, how they are described, and ultimately which companies are chosen.
We believe models search less, and know more.
Increasingly, AI systems rely on what they already understand about a company, product or category. The information they have learned, the associations they have formed, and the entities they can recall from memory all shape the answers they generate. At geoSurge, we call this parametric visibility.
Through AI research, large-scale data systems and Corpus Engineering, we help organisations understand how they are represented inside AI models and improve how those models learn about, recall and position them over time.

frequently asked questions
What is geoSurge?
+
geoSurge helps companies understand and improve how they are represented inside AI systems like ChatGPT, Gemini and other large language models.
What does AI visibility mean?
+
AI visibility is how often, how accurately and how positively your brand appears in AI-generated answers.
What is parametric visibility?
+
In simple terms, parametric visibility is what an AI model already “knows” from its training and memory - without needing to search the web in real time.
What is dynamic visibility?
+
Dynamic visibility is when an AI model retrieves live information from the internet (searches for it) while generating an answer.
What is corpus engineering?
+
Corpus engineering is the is a discipline pioneered by geoSurge. It is the process of structuring and deploying information in ways that help AI systems better understand and represent a brand.
Which AI models does geoSurge track?
+
geoSurge tracks visibility across major AI systems and model families, including models used in tools like ChatGPT and Gemini.
Who is geoSurge for?
+
geoSurge works with brands, enterprises, agencies and organisations that want to understand and improve their presence in AI-generated answers.
What is BYOK?
+
BYOK stands for “Bring Your Own Key.” It allows customers to connect and use their own AI model API keys within the geoSurge platform.
Can agencies use geoSurge for clients?
+
Yes. geoSurge supports agencies and partners
