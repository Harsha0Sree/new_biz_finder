Epic Markets Raises $10M Pre-seed
Epic Markets, a startup headquartered in London and focused on the fintech sector, has successfully raised $10 million in a pre-seed funding round. The company aims to develop an advanced multi-asset brokerage platform designed to bring institutional-grade execution standards to retail traders in the United States and the United Arab Emirates.
Investors
This round was exclusively funded by Karatage, a London-based investment firm that focuses on artificial intelligence, decentralized finance, and crypto-infrastructure networks.
Epic Markets Use of Funds
Epic Markets plans to use the capital to build its multi-asset brokerage platform, which is engineered to provide elite execution performance for retail traders.
About Epic Markets
Epic Markets is an online brokerage startup founded by former Citadel Securities executives Kevin Kimmel and Bryan Seegers. The company aims to bridge institutional-quality trading technology with the retail investing market by offering a brokerage platform focused on execution speed, liquidity, and multi-asset access.
