fonio.ai Raises $17M Seed at $140M Valuation
fonio.ai, a Vienna, Austria-based AI-powered communication platform for small and medium-sized businesses. The company has raised $17 million in a seed round at a $140 million valuation, bringing its total funding to over $20 million.
Investors
The round was led by 20VC, a global venture capital firm. Additional participation included existing investors as well as founders and executives from Synthesia, HubSpot, and Revolut.
fonio.ai Use of Funds
Capital will be used to accelerate the development of the company AI agent, expand its international presence by opening new offices in New York, Munich, Milan, Paris, London, and Warsaw, and continue building its team.
About fonio.ai
Founded in 2024 by Daniel Keinrath and Matthias Gruber, fonio.ai provides an AI-native operating system for SMBs that automates customer communication across voice, WhatsApp, and other channels. The platform is designed to replace fragmented legacy tools with a unified, end-to-end AI-first communication infrastructure.
