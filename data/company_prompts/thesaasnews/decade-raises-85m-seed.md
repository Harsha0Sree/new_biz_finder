Decade Raises $85M Seed
Decade, a Sao Paulo, Brazil-based provider of an AI-native wealth advisory platform, has raised $85 million in Seed funding. The company offers a financial services platform that combines artificial intelligence with human expertise to provide personalized wealth management solutions.
Investors
Participants in the round included Greenoaks, Benchmark, and Diffusion.
Decade Use of Funds
The company intends to use the capital to expand its operations and accelerate the development of its platform.
About Decade
Founded by CEO Vitor Olivier and Felipe Meneses, Decade provides an AI-native financial services platform. Their solution pairs each client with a senior human advisor and a proprietary AI model that monitors daily spending and investment portfolios to deliver personalized financial guidance.
