## Funding Article Content
Seed Round
GreenVibe Raises $12M Seed
GreenVibe raises $12 million in seed funding to expand its AI-powered concrete structural health monitoring platform for the global construction industry.
GreenVibe Raises $12M Seed
GreenVibe, based in Ramat Gan, Israel, provides a construction technology platform that uses embedded wireless sensors and AI to provide real-time concrete structural health data. The company has raised $12 million in funding to date across its seed rounds to support its growth and expand its concrete verification technology.
Investors
The round was backed by Insight Partners, Tidhar Construction, Israel-Canada, and Shikun and Binui.
GreenVibe Use of Funds
GreenVibe plans to use the capital to support its corporate growth, expand its market reach to international contractors and developers, and advance its efforts to integrate its technology into the ASTM and Eurocode standards frameworks.
About GreenVibe
GreenVibe develops AI-powered wireless sensors that are embedded in concrete before pouring to monitor strength and structural health in real time. Combining five sensing technologies, including ultrasound, the platform delivers concrete strength and status updates every 30 minutes through mobile and desktop applications. GreenVibe has more than 10,000 sensors deployed across over 20 construction projects and laboratories.
