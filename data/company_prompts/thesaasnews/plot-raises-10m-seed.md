Plot Raises $10M Seed

Plot Raises $10M Seed
Plot, a New York-based social video intelligence startup, has announced a $10M seed funding round to scale its AI-powered marketing platform for enterprise brands. This latest injection of capital brings the company's total funding to $14M.
Investors
The funding round was co-led by XYZ Venture Capital and Mischief Ventures, with participation from Seven Seven Six and Acme Capital.
Plot Use of Funds
Plot plans to use the new capital to meet rising enterprise demand for its platform and to expand its suite of AI-powered marketing workflows. This includes the rollout of agentic products for creator sourcing and deep consumer research.
About Plot
Founded in 2024, Plot provides an agentic marketing platform designed for the video era. The platform uses AI intelligence to process and analyze social video content, enabling enterprise teams to track campaigns, uncover consumer insights, and manage creator relationships even when content is not explicitly tagged by users.
