## Funding Article Content
Pre-seed Round
June AI Raises $20M Pre-seed
June AI raises $20M pre-seed round led by TIME Ventures to automate enterprise software implementation and AI deployment for large organizations.
June AI Raises $20M Pre-seed
June AI, based in New York, is a technology startup focused on reinventing enterprise software implementation for the AI era. The company announced it has emerged from stealth with $20 million in pre-seed funding to help organizations modernize and configure their business systems using autonomous AI agents.
Investors
The funding round was led by Marc Benioff’s TIME Ventures, with participation from Michael Dell, Diane Greene, Aaron Levie, George Kurtz, SV Angel, Conviction Embed, Abstract, A*, and Vesey Ventures.
June AI Use of Funds
June AI plans to use the capital to scale its platform, which uses AI agents to automate the implementation, migration, and adoption of enterprise software, aiming to reduce technical debt and accelerate AI transformation for large organizations.
About June AI
Founded in 2026, June AI was established by CEO Efrat Rapoport, CTO Idan Tsitiat, President Barak Goldstein, and Chief Architect Ohad Hen. The team previously founded Bonobo AI, which was acquired by Salesforce in 2019. The company provides an AI-driven platform that integrates with existing enterprise stacks to automate manual system configurations and deployments.
