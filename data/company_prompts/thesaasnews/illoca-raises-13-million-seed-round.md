Illoca Use of Funds
The company will use the funding to advance its AI-native architectural design engine, expand product development, and redefine professional design workflows through intelligent AI interfaces for architects.
About Illoca
Founded by Chin-Yi Cheng and Chiaowei Yu, Illoca is an AI-native design engine focused on transforming architectural workflows through intelligent collaboration tools. The company’s flagship product, Illoca Tracing Paper™ interface, enables architects to interact with AI using sketches, markups, and natural language to generate 2D and 3D designs in real time. Headquartered in San Ramon, California, Illoca’s vision is to build a direct conduit for your intent and taste. Early adopters include Kajima Corporation.

How do Tracing Paper and Plamo differ from generic AI or conventional design tools?
Tracing Paper and Plamo are AI-native design tools built for the controllability required by professional practice. Together they bridge the gap between creative exploration and technical production:
Beyond Image-Based AI
: Unlike generic tools that produce static pixels, our tools understand architectural intent and generate structured, parametric 2D and 3D schematics that are design-ready.
Enhancing Conventional Workflows
: While conventional CAD and BIM software often require manual, high-effort translation of ideas, our tools accept natural inputs—sketches, photos, and texts—to generate design-ready results directly, so your effort goes into judgment rather than execution.
How do they work with my BIM/CAD workflow?
Tracing Paper
is an intelligent canvas that replaces the traditional hundred-button toolset with a workflow that mirrors how design studios actually work: sketching, marking up, and pinning ideas. It supports two modes:
Ideate
, where a rough sketch or verbal prompt becomes an immediate entry point for architectural drawings; and
Iterate
, where you refine concepts through redlines and markups while the system updates geometry and documentation in real-time. It is designed to be your
"Thinking Interface,"
allowing you to export high-fidelity work directly into Autodesk Revit once your design is set.
Plamo
is a cross-disciplinary modeling tool that turns text, photos, or plans into fully editable, parametric 3D models in any browser. You can easily iterate and experiment with concepts and ideas, then download them as Rhino, Grasshopper, or GLB files in organized layers that you can keep editing in other professional software.
