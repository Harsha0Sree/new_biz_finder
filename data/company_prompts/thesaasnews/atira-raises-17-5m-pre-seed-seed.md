Atira Raises $17.5 Million To Build AI Orchestration Platform For Industrial Sales Engineering
Atira has raised
$17.5 million in funding
, including a
$15 million Seed round led by Accel
and a previously unannounced $2.5 million Pre-Seed round. UVC, Fortino and Booom also participated, alongside industry executives and operators including Ann-Kristin Achleitner, Whirlpool CEO Marc Bitzer, Markus Flik and Celonis Co-Founder and Co-CEO Bastian Nominacher.
The Munich-based company plans to use the financing to expand its go-to-market organization, accelerate product development and support international growth.
Founded in November 2024 by
Florian Diegruber and August DuMont Schütte
, Atira is building a multi-agent AI platform designed to automate industrial sales engineering workflows for companies selling complex, built-to-order products and services.
Diegruber previously served as a Commercial Lead at Palantir, while DuMont Schütte was an ML Software Engineer at Google.
Atira’s platform helps industrial companies process complex requests for quotation, or RFQs, by reviewing specifications, identifying and commenting on key requirements, configuring appropriate solutions and producing bid documentation.
The software connects with existing CRM, ERP and CPQ systems and can generate outputs including technical documentation, configuration proposals, pricing options, completed customer templates and final proposals.
Atira is targeting a process that remains highly manual across industrial companies. Preparing a single proposal can take weeks or months and often requires coordination among sales engineers, technical teams, legal teams and commercial staff reviewing hundreds or thousands of pages of specifications.
The company estimates that industrial sales engineering represents more than
$128 billion in annual labor spending globally
.
Since its commercial launch in November 2025, Atira has signed more than
15 customers
, including ABB E-Mobility, Chiron Group, Rema Tip Top and Robel.
Atira said early customers have reduced request-to-bid preparation times by as much as
80%
, while using the platform to standardize proposal processes, preserve institutional knowledge and allow engineering teams to spend more time on higher-value technical work.
The company is also seeing expansion within existing customer accounts as deployments move beyond initial use cases.
Atira plans to broaden its platform into adjacent commercial workflows including
pricing intelligence, aftersales and supplier coordination
as it works toward becoming a broader operating system for industrial commercial teams.

The company
AGO GmbH Energie + Anlagen, a subsidiary of RheinEnergie, is a plant engineering company specializing in the planning and construction of highly efficient energy supply systems, including combined heat and power (CHP) units, large-scale industrial heat pumps, absorption chillers, and biomass heating plants, as well as ventilation and air conditioning systems. Through AGO Caldora, the company operates a dedicated division that manufactures large-scale high-temperature heat pumps.
Founded in 1980 and headquartered in Kulmbach, the company plans, engineers, builds, and services energy plants for industrial and municipal customers, from dairies and breweries to utilities and district heating operators.

02
The challenge
02
The challenge
AGO's systems are not off-the-shelf products. Every project is a complex, individually engineered solution, designed manufacturer-independently around each customer's specific processes, energy demand, site conditions, and economic targets. As a result, no two customer requests are alike, and each one must be individually configured, dimensioned, and calculated before AGO can decide how to respond and which solution to propose.
The tenders AGO responds to are correspondingly heavy. A single public tender can arrive as an entire document package with thousands of individual requirements spread across specifications, plans, contract terms, and standards documents. Interpreting them correctly requires deep knowledge of AGO's technology portfolio, feasibility boundaries, regulatory requirements, and commercial standards, knowledge that is distributed across departments such as process engineering, EMSR, construction, service, and commercial teams. Working through these packages manually in Excel and PDF meant substantial sorting and coordination effort, and critical deviations, extra-cost items, or infeasible requirements risked being identified late or missed entirely, with direct consequences for margins and proposal quality.
‍
03
Solutions & Outcomes
03
Solution & Outcomes
The Solution
AGO uses Atira to run its tender response process from incoming request to bid. Complete tender packages are transferred into Atira as received, with folder structures preserved, and processed entirely within Atira's closed, secure environment. All documents and data remain confidential inside the system. There, Atira automatically extracts and structures every individual requirement by discipline and routes it to the right department, from process engineering and EMSR to commercial and service teams, so specialists only see what is relevant to them. For every requirement, Atira suggests a proposal and confidence level, so engineers start from a qualified proposal instead of a blank page. Deviations from AGO's standard and requirements that trigger extra costs are flagged and commented directly in the tool, and structured deviation lists and Excel exports feed straight into AGO's quoting process.
Nothing Slips Through
AGO's core requirement was completeness: in tenders with thousands of requirements, a single overlooked clause can become an expensive obligation. Atira treats every sentence of the tender as a potential requirement and evaluates it, including side clauses that would previously have been skimmed over. Instead of working through documents superficially under time pressure, the team now works through a structured, prioritized list where critical points are surfaced early and every open item is visible.
From Thousands of Requirements to a Focused Workload
Atira's pre-sorting lets AGO reduce tender revisions down to the key aspects. Sales and proposal teams resolve the large majority of ToDos themselves using Atira's suggestions and AGO's captured knowledge, and only the genuinely difficult cases are escalated to specialist engineers, keeping scarce expert capacity focused on the decisions that determine the bid.

The company

02
The challenge
02
The challenge
Automotive RFQs often contain highly sensitive information: OEM-specific standards, early-stage product concepts, technical drawings, validation requirements, commercial assumptions, supplier scopes, and project timelines. For Osbra, this information must be handled with the same level of care, confidentiality, and traceability as any other customer-controlled project data.
At the same time, every OEM works with its own standards, factory norms, specification formats, and approval processes. A single RFQ can include dozens or even hundreds of files, ranging from technical drawings and product specifications to Excel sheets, test requirements, and special customer concepts.
Before Osbra can respond, the team needs to understand the full scope, identify relevant norms, assess technical feasibility, estimate testing costs, and surface potential red flags. This review requires experienced employees across sales, engineering, quality, purchasing, and project management.
The process was time-consuming because relevant information was spread across large document packages. Critical requirements could be hidden deep inside attachments, tables, or customer-specific standards. When subcontractors were involved, the complexity increased further: scope had to be extracted, supplier-relevant information prepared, and cost assumptions tracked separately - without unnecessarily sharing complete customer documentation.
Osbra needed a faster and more controlled way to evaluate RFQs while maintaining the confidentiality, security, and care expected in automotive supplier relationships.

Solutions & Outcomes

Solution & Outcomes
The Solution
Together with Atira, Osbra introduced an AI-supported RFQ evaluation process designed for secure, Osbra-controlled processing of customer requirements.
Incoming RFQ documents are analyzed within a structured internal workflow. Dedicated Atira agents support specific evaluation steps: identifying relevant norms, extracting test requirements, preparing cost inputs, prefilling product specification sheets, flagging red flags, and creating structured handover material for internal experts.
The process helps Osbra turn complex RFQ packages into actionable internal working documents - without replacing expert judgment. Employees remain in control of the final assessment, customer communication, and bid/no-bid decision.
Secure Processing for Highly Sensitive Automotive Requirements

The purpose is to turn complex RFQ packages into structured internal working documents - with clear traceability back to the original customer materials.
Access to sensitive information is controlled by Osbra’s internal workflow and permission logic. The Atira Agents support the preparation of structured working documents, while Osbra’s experts remain responsible for validation, customer communication, and final decisions.
Where subcontractors or suppliers need to be involved, Atira helps create focused handover extracts so that only the relevant scope information is shared - not the full customer document package. This reduces unnecessary data exposure while improving clarity for everyone involved.
In this way, Osbra can accelerate RFQ evaluation while meeting the confidentiality, traceability, and security expectations of automotive customers.
