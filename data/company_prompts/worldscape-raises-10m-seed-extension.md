Developer FAQs
How does the Entity Component System work?
The Entity Component System (ECS) is a software architectural pattern designed for scalability, modularity, and performance in complex simulations. In ECS, entities represent individual objects (e.g., aircraft, satellites, airports, and assets), serving as placeholders for their associated functionality. These entities consist of components, which are modular data structures that define specific attributes or behaviors, such as speed, sensors, health, or AI-driven logic. Systems operate on components, applying rules, updates, or processes (e.g., movement calculations and communication status updates) to facilitate dynamic interactions between entities. This design allows ECS to manage and scale simulations involving millions of entities efficiently, as systems can process only the relevant components without unnecessary overhead.
I hear you have a Worldscape Marketplace?
The Marketplace facilitates the standardization, sharing, and monetization of entities, data sources, AI models, agents, 3D models, imagery, and geospatial data among organizations. It establishes a robust business model that enables partners to securely bring their intellectual property to the platform while ensuring compliance with privacy regulations. This enables customers to leverage the platform's world-class commercial capabilities and utilize its existing customer resources.
How do I manage scale with Worldscape?
The Worldscape platform is built on a containerized, distributed compute fabric based on Microsoft Orleans, which enables the deployment of virtual machines and containers. Orleans is open-source, powers Microsoft XBOX Live and Azure, and integrates with Agentic AI. Therefore, it can be deployed across all major commercial clouds, including Microsoft Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud Platform, as well as to government-secure or private clouds and edge computing locations. This enables faster and more secure processing, as well as 3D rendering closer to the clients consuming the platform, thereby increasing performance and scale while providing maximum flexibility to deploy capabilities anywhere.
Why is a physics-based game engine needed for simulation?
A physics-based game engine is ideal for simulation because it models real-world forces, such as gravity, collisions, and friction, enabling dynamic, interactive, and realistic behavior. It’s scalable, efficient, and adaptable—perfect for training, prototyping, or predictive modeling across various industries, including robotics, engineering, and entertainment.

Quick Answers
What problem does Worldscape solve?
Modern commercial and military scenarios are complex, dynamic, and prone to disruption. Furthermore, customers are facing a world where systems, data sources, agents, bots, and sensors are distributed across the enterprise as high-speed fiber, 5G, and satellite networks deliver a constantly connected world.
The Worldscape Data Fabric securely connects these distributed components, using a single query language, making it easy to orchestrate intelligent actions for your business. Furthermore, AI requests oceans of data to infer intelligent actions.
Worldscape trains AI via specific simulations at a fraction of the speed and cost. Traditional AI simulation solutions offer a static model with narrowly targeted snapshot data; Worldscape provides a common operating picture, creating an Agentic and Autonomous control plane that not only allows you to visualize and model, but also simulate, analyze, and orchestrate your processes in real time at scale with simplicity, providing Decision Advantage.
How is Worldscape Different from existing digital twins?
Worldscape is different because it allows you to ingest multiple diverse data feeds and then use agents that trigger:
Agentic Automation:
Embedded decision-making agents that can autonomously execute plans and remediation.
Autonomous Workflows:
Simulations trigger chained agent actions, enabling self-optimizing operations.
Unified Model & Simulation:
Consolidates real-world physical constraints such as Power, Space, Network, Cooling in a datacenter with logistical parameters (Loads, Modes, Nodes, Roads) in a single platform.
Who benefits from Worldscape?
Built as a modular platform, Worldscape benefits several roles across your business, including:
System Operators gain real-time visibility and automated recommendations.
Decision Specialists leverage advanced, “what if” simulations for strategic planning.
Sustainability Leads monitor and minimize carbon impact.
IT & Facility Managers optimize operational resource usage at scale.
And a host of other roles.
What are the core features?
Core features include:
Live Data Ingestion & Real-time Monitoring:
Connectors for IoT sensors and cameras, ERP events, and on-wire connected data.
Multi-Agent Orchestration:
Configurable AI agents for predictive alerts, simulation triggers, and autonomous task execution.
Scenario Builder:
Drag and drop interface to define constraints, KPIs, and high-definition simulation models.
Rapid Cognition:
Visualization for critical scenarios with data at scale for an “agent boss”, encompassing agents, for example, on probability scoring, cycle time reduction, carbon footprint, risk analytics, and capacity utilization.
Auto Mitigation Engine:
Rule-based, agent-monitored triggers and agent-driven remediation workflows.
Autonomy Training Environment:
Virtual, physically accurate, 3D environments for training AI Agents, autonomous vehicles, robotics, and humanoid machines.
Multi-Level Security:
RBAC and ABAC combine to enable multiple users to view, update, and interact with the platform at the appropriate security level.
Single Query Language:
developers and agents can read and write to the world’s data using a single query language, dramatically simplifying development.
How does modeling & simulation work?
The Worldscape platform is a unified control environment that combines real-time data ingestion, simulation, and AI decision support.
Define Constraints:
Specify physical limits (e.g., power, cooling capacity, rack space) and logistical parameters (e.g., load volumes, transport modes).
Real-Physics Game Engine Core:
Leverage a real-physics simulation layer—similar to those in advanced game engines—to accurately model forces, collisions, material properties, and dynamic interactions. This ensures that virtual assets obey Newtonian mechanics, thermal dynamics, and fluid flow, providing a one-to-one correspondence with real-world behavior.
Parallel Scenario Execution:
Run thousands of digital experiments in parallel, each leveraging the game engine’s physics solver to predict structural stresses, vibration effects, airflow patterns, and energy consumption across data center and transportation networks.
Federated Simulation:
Integrate existing multiple simulations, models, data, agents, and behaviors within the Worldscape open platform. Simulations run on a common Entity Component System environment.

The Secure Agentic AI Platform
Ultimate Security – the Platform that Migrates
to You
Enterprise and government organizations have invested years and millions of dollars in building on-premises systems, with the explicit intent of maintaining a secure infrastructure that remains under their control. However, these closed systems are inherently challenged when it comes to leveraging the power of AI, which is, by definition, a cloud-intensive frontier. That's why Worldscape is built differently - our technology is embedded within your infrastructure, allowing you to maintain your existing, hard-fought security practices while providing access to the unmatched power and opportunity of Agentic AI.
Security Implementation
Platform security is implemented via defense-in-depth and utilizes a zero-trust architecture inside and out.
Secure development practices following SDLC, including deployment pipelines, secure dependency management, static analysis, signed builds, and role audits.
TLS everywhere - all internal and external services utilize encrypted and authenticated channels.
Certificate-based and EntraID-managed identity authentication for system components.
Integration with enterprise Auth0 for RBAC and SSO.
At rest, encryption is required in all storage systems, with optional customer-supplied keys.
Information security management program across the company, including leadership and operations. SOC2 Type I certified.
Privacy-based controls integrated at the system level - PII and user data identified and protected.
Hardware Security Modules, where applicable, vaulted credentials for internal and external systems.
Customer data segmentation - tenant-based management of data.
Vulnerability Reporting
We welcome external vulnerability and security reports and are evaluating the establishment of a formal bug bounty program. If you are a security researcher or an interested user, we'd love to talk to you!

Privacy Implementation
Worldscape can help your organization address critical privacy requirements regardless of jurisdiction. Applications built within the Worldscape platform automatically utilize the following capabilities, and appropriate data is available via our APIs to data privacy groups and Data Protection Officers.
Consent Management: Consent management can be tracked by users
Records of Processing: Identification of processing function and automatic cataloging/categorization of systems
PII Protection: Automatic tokenization to remove or reduce PII within the system
Data Subject Rights Workflow: Automatic anonymization and deletion of data is available
